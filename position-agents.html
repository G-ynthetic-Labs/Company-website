Public safety · Position Agents

An AI helper for every position in the emergency center. A person behind every order.

When a disaster hits, a city opens its Emergency Operations Center (EOC): a room where staff in set positions coordinate the response. Position Agents give each position its own AI helper. It reads that position's information, drafts its reports and suggests next steps. A named person approves every order.
