Public safety · Position Agents

An assistant for every EOC position. A person behind every order.

One assistant per Emergency Operations Center position. It reads that position's own sources, drafts its ICS forms and recommends orders. A named person in the named role approves every one, in every mode.

Discuss a pilotSee City SeCUReD

Simulated only · no screen yet

What each assistant does

Drafts and recommends. Never decides.

Positions are named the way NIMS and ICS name them: Chief, Officer, Director, Supervisor and Leader. Resources are Available, Assigned or Out-of-Service.

01

Reads its own sources

Each position reaches only its own sources, read-only. When a fact was true is kept apart from when it was fetched, and stale data is stated, never hidden.

02

Drafts ICS products

Seven ICS forms drafted from what the position knows, every one marked as an exercise when it is one.

03

Recommends orders

Orders are checked against the position's authority. No rule ever approves an order: a named person does.

04

Builds the common picture

The EOC Director's assistant assembles the common operating picture and computes its blind spots, rather than asserting them.

05

Checks span of control

Thirteen positions, with span-of-control checks against NIMS guidance, so an overloaded organization is a finding, not a footnote.

06

Alerts drafted, never sent

A public alert is only ever drafted, and only with its status set to Exercise.

Where it fits

The AI in City SeCUReD's seats.

In a City SeCUReD exercise, any of the 13 positions can be filled by a person, by a position agent or by a fixed doctrine policy, and the runs can be compared on identical scenarios. That is how a jurisdiction can test an assistant before it trusts one.
