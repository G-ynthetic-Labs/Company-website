About

An AI research company building governed AI.

We develop a computing discipline for governed AI: a way to structure any field so that an AI working in it is controlled, remembers what it did, and leaves a record of everything it tried. Our products are its first applications.

Why we exist

Preparation matters most when it's tested.

We believe that with responsible, accountable governance over what AI is allowed to do, we can build technology that helps the people responsible for keeping us safe be better prepared for the moments when preparation matters most.

We can't control when disaster comes. We can change what happens when it does.

Principle

People sign; agents don't.

Every approval, order and signature belongs to a named person.

Principle

Say what was checked.

Plain words, honest status, and no claims we can't prove.

Our story

From structural welding to AI architecture.

Our founder came to AI from structural welding. Welding teaches load paths and structural integrity, and that is how we approach AI: structure first, so it holds under weight.
