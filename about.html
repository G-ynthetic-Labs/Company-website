About

See how the hardest problems connect. Act on them in time.

G-ynthetic Labs is an independent research and development company building tools that help people see how the hardest problems of the AI age connect, and act on them in time. Every agent we build is controlled, remembers what it did, and leaves a record of everything it tried.

Why we started

The next generations are inheriting the hardest problems, with the least time to solve them.

Climate, an autonomous workforce and what it means to be human are arriving together. We build tools to see how they connect and act at their scale, with AI doing the groundwork and people deciding.

Principle

People sign; agents don't.

Every approval, order and signature belongs to a named person.

Principle

Say what was checked.

Plain words, honest status, and no claims we can't prove.
