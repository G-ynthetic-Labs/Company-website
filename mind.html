A companion that remembers.

G-ynthetic Mind is an AI companion with long-term memory. It picks up where you left off, finds what you told it weeks ago, and helps you turn goals into steps.

What it does

Memory first.

01

Long-term memory

It remembers across conversations and finds things by text, meaning, tag or the people and things involved.

02

Goals into steps

Tell it what you want to get done and it breaks the goal into steps you can follow.
