Business · Desk Agents

It reads the mail and drafts the reply. You press send.

A family of agents for the desks that run a small business. They sort, summarise and draft from answers you approved. Money, legal, personal-data and threat signals go straight to a person with nothing drafted. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

The desks

Five desks, one set of rules.

For you

Inbox Assistant

Sorts, summarises and drafts replies for one person's inbox. Show original reveals every byte of a message and its fingerprint.

Online shops

Customer Desk

Turns customer mail into tickets, keeps suggestions and complaints apart on a board by topic, and drafts from approved answers.

Any appointment business

Booking Desk

Code finds free times, holds them and drafts the offer. An appointment is booked only when a person approves its confirmation.

Repair garages

Garage Desk

Bookings handled; quotes, approvals of work, breakdowns, warranty and courtesy cars always go to a person.

Tire and oil shops

Tire & Oil Desk

Quick-service bookings; tire prices, stock, stored tires and fleet accounts always go to a person.

The trades

Paperwork

From job notes to estimates, work orders and invoices, checked against your forms, price book, permits and credentials. Only a person signs.

Same rules on every desk

The model never routes and never prices.

Rules

Rules are data

Where each message goes is decided by rules you can read. When no rule fits, it goes to a person.

Approval

Exact bytes approved

Every reply is approved over its exact content. What you approved is what goes out.

Assistant

It types, you press

The in-app assistant can type into fields. It has no way to press a button.

Screen

One familiar screen

A webmail layout that fits one window, light and dark, with a phone layout.
