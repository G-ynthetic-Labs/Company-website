Business · Desk Agents

It reads the mail and drafts the reply. You press send.

Desk Agents are ready-made AI agents for small businesses. They read incoming email, sort it, summarise it and write draft replies from answers you've approved. Anything about money, legal matters or risk goes straight to a person. Nothing is sent without you.
