Flagship · Build-A-Bot

Build your own AI agent. It can't go rogue.

Build-A-Bot lets anyone assemble an agent from modular parts, the way you'd build a bear at a workshop. Every bot inherits the same rules automatically: it asks before it acts, and it keeps a record of everything.

Join early accessSee plans
How it works

Five steps from an idea to an agent at work.

Pick a role

Start from a role in our library: a booking desk, a service writer, a billing clerk.

Add skills and tools

Choose what it can do and what it can read, from parts that are already built and tested.

Add its paperwork

Scan your own forms and it learns to fill them in.

Set its rules

Decide what it may do on its own and what it must ask you about.

It goes to work

It drafts, fills and prepares. You approve anything that matters.

Form Bench

Turn any paper form into something an agent can fill.

Photograph a form, such as a repair order. Mark its fields, fill it in, and save a fillable PDF plus a template your agent uses from then on.

Flow Bench

Lay out the job, then check it before it runs.

Draw the agent's steps, place forms into them and assign each step to an agent. Built-in checks confirm every step has an agent, every high-risk step sits behind a person's approval, and every form has instructions.

Why it's different

Most agent builders let you assemble anything. Ours won't let a bot go rogue.

n-HR™ · non-Human Resources

Hire agents the way you hire people.

Behind Build-A-Bot is n-HR™, non-Human Resources: a department that sits right next to HR and works the same way, so your company already knows how to run it. Every agent starts from a job description, a role, its skills and its tools, the same way a new hire starts from a posting. The library grows with every bot built, so each new agent starts further along than the last. More about n-HR™.
