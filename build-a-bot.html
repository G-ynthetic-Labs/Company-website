Why it's different

Most agent builders let you assemble anything. Ours won't let a bot go rogue.

Typical agent builder

  • Acts as soon as the model decides
  • Reads whatever it can reach
  • Guesses when unsure
  • Logs, if you set them up
  • Runs in the vendor's cloud

Build-A-Bot

  • Acts within your rules, or asks
  • Reads only approved sources
  • Asks a person. Silence is never a yes
  • Tamper-evident record of every attempt
  • Runs on your own computer
