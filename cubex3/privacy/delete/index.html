Delete your account

Applies to: CUBEX³ (com.gyntheticlabs.cubex3), published by G-ynthetic Labs.

CUBEX³ has no accounts and no servers. Everything the game stores — your commander profiles, their experience, your campaign, your settings — is written to your own device and never sent anywhere.

That means there is no account to delete, no request to send us, and no waiting period. Deletion is immediate and entirely in your hands.

Delete everything the app has stored

Either of these removes all of it, permanently:

From Android settings

  1. Open Settings — Apps — CUBEX³.
  2. Choose Storage.
  3. Choose Clear data (sometimes called Clear storage).

Or simply uninstall

Uninstalling CUBEX³ removes the same data along with the app.

Both are irreversible. We hold no copy, so nothing can be restored afterwards — please be sure before you do it.

What gets deleted

  • Every commander profile and its experience
  • Your campaign progress, doctrine points and unlocked armies
  • Your chosen army, view preferences and settings
  • Any record the app kept of tutorials you have seen

That is the complete list, because it is everything the app stores.

What we hold about you: nothing

We operate no server for CUBEX³ and have no database. We cannot identify you, cannot see your games, and hold no record to erase. If you would like that confirmed in writing, email G-ynthetic-Labs@proton.me and we will send you a short statement saying so, within 30 days.

The advertising identifier

CUBEX³ is free and shows one banner advertisement, served by Google AdMob. To do that, Google's ad SDK uses your device's advertising ID. That identifier belongs to Google and your device, not to us — we never receive it and cannot delete it for you.

You control it directly:

  • Android Settings — Privacy — Ads lets you reset or delete your

advertising ID at any time.

  • In the EEA and the UK, the consent choice shown before the first ad can be

reopened from the app's account panel.

Deleting the app's data as described above does not reset that identifier, and resetting that identifier does not delete the app's data. They are separate controls, which is why both are listed here.
