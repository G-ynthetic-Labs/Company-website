Delete your account

Applies to: CUBEX³ (com.gyntheticlabs.cubex3), published by G-ynthetic Labs.

You can delete your CUBEX³ account and everything stored against it at any time. There are two ways to do it, and both remove the same things.

Delete it from inside the app

This is the fastest route and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open CUBEX³.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then choose Delete permanently.

Your account is erased as soon as that completes. You are signed out and any active subscription is cancelled in the same step.

Ask us to delete it

If you cannot open the app — you have changed device, uninstalled it, or lost access to your sign-in — email G-ynthetic-Labs@proton.me from the email address on the account, with the subject Delete my account.

We will confirm the request, delete the account, and reply to confirm it is done. We respond within 30 days and normally much sooner. We may need to ask one question to confirm the account is yours; we will not ask you for your password.

What gets deleted

Deleted immediately and permanently:

  • Your account and its sign-in identity
  • Your email address, display name, and profile image reference
  • Your saved campaign in the cloud, and every save slot attached to the account
  • The record of which device last synced
  • Your customer record with our payment processor, including the email held

against it

Any active subscription is cancelled as part of the deletion, so you are not billed again.

What is not deleted, and why

The campaign saved on your own device. Deleting your cloud account does not reach into your phone. That data is yours and stays where it is — uninstalling CUBEX³ removes it.

Payment and invoice records held by our payment processor. Tax and accounting law requires financial records of a completed transaction to be retained for a set period. Those records are kept by the processor under that obligation, not by us for our own purposes, and they are removed when the retention period ends.

Retention

There is no waiting period and no soft-delete. Once deletion completes, the account cannot be recovered and neither can the campaign that was synced to it. Backup copies of our databases are cycled out on a rolling 30-day schedule.

If you sign in again afterwards, you get a new, empty account.
