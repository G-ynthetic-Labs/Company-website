In plain terms

AI can now do real work on its own. We build the structure that keeps it safe.

What an AI agent isSoftware that does a job for you: answers email, fills in forms, drafts reports
The riskMost agents act without asking and leave no clear record of what they did
What we buildAgents that ask a person first and record every step, like inspected work on a job site
The difference

Usually nobody can see what an AI agent did, or why.

Typical AI agents

  • Act without asking
  • Forget earlier work
  • Leave no clear record

Our agents

  • Ask a person before anything important
  • Remember what they did
  • Record every step they try
One engine

Every product runs on the same safety system.

We call it the engine. A city practising for a flood and a two-person repair shop answering email get the same checks.
