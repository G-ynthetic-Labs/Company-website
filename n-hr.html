n-HR™ · non-Human Resources

Your company already knows how to run this.

n-HR™ sits in your company right next to HR, and it works the same way. Agents are hired against a job description, given only the access their role needs, supervised by a named person and kept on file. Nothing new to learn: if your company can run HR, it can run n-HR.

Simple to adopt

Nothing new to learn. You already know how.

Most AI rollouts stall because nobody knows who owns the agents or what rules they follow. n-HR answers that with the system every company already runs.

01

Familiar from day one

Job descriptions, roles, access, sign-off, files, reviews. Your managers already know every step.

02

Clear ownership

Every agent has a role and a named person it answers to, the same way every employee has a manager.

03

Fits your existing structure

n-HR is one more department beside HR. Your policies, approvals and chain of command carry over.

Runs the way HR does

Everything HR does for a person, n-HR does for an agent.

The engine underneath every agent was built to work like an HR department: nobody starts without a job, nobody gets access they don't need, and everything is on file.
