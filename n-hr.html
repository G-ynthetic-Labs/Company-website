n-HR™ · non-Human Resources

Your company already knows how to run this.

As companies put AI agents to work, someone has to manage them. n-HR™ (non-Human Resources) is a department for your AI agents that sits next to HR and works the same way. If your company can run HR, it can run n-HR.
