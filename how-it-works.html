How it works

Agents draft. People decide.

Most agent systems let the model choose what happens next. Ours take that choice away from the model and give it back to the people accountable for the outcome.

Four rules, every agent

Every agent we make, and every bot you build, follows the same four rules.

01

Permission first

An agent acts only when your rules allow it or a named person approves it.

02

Approved sources only

It reads only the sources you approved, nothing else it happens to reach.

03

Asks a person

When no rule covers a situation, it asks. Silence is never a yes.

04

Records everything

Every attempt, approval and refusal goes into a tamper-evident record you can export.

The loop

Propose, approve, record, replay.

Propose

The agent drafts the reply, the form or the order, and says what it wants to do.

Approve

Your rules allow it, or a named person approves the exact content. The assistant can type; it can't press a button.

Record

What happened, who decided and when go into a hash-chained, append-only record.

Replay

Runs can be reproduced and checked later, by you or by someone who doesn't trust you.

What it never does

Lines our agents don't cross.

  • Never sends, signs or approves on its own. In any mode.
  • Never routes work by the model's judgement. Routing is fixed rules with stated reasons.
  • Never stores credentials. Keys are referenced, never written, logged or echoed.
  • Never commands a device in our public-safety products. They are read-only.
  • Never sends your data home. There is no network code in the engine's core.
  • Never claims more than it proves. The record is tamper-evident, and we make no compliance claims.
