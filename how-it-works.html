How it works

AI prepares the work. People decide.

These are the rules every one of our AI agents follows, in every product. The short version: the AI can prepare work, but it can't act on anything important until a person says yes.

Four rules, every agent

Four rules, for every agent we make and every one you build.

Permission firstBlocked from everything until allowed
Approved information onlyReads only what you allow
Asks a personNo answer means no, never yes
Records everythingEvery attempt, approval and refusal
How one piece of work moves

Prepare, approve, record, check.

  1. 01PrepareThe agent drafts the work
  2. 02ApproveA set rule or a named person says yes
  3. 03RecordKept in a log where any change shows
  4. 04CheckAnyone can re-run it later
Compared with the usual way

Who makes the decision.

A typical AI agent

  • The AI decides what to do
  • Allowed unless something blocks it
  • Keeps logs only if set up
  • Hard to re-run and check
  • Runs on the vendor's servers

Our agents

  • A named person decides
  • Blocked unless allowed
  • A record where any change shows
  • Re-runs to exactly the same result
  • Runs on your computer, with your choice of AI
What it never does

Lines our agents don't cross.

Never signs or sends on its own
Never lets the AI choose who handles what
Never stores your passwords
Never controls a machine or device
Never sends your data to us
Never claims more than it can show
